# Phone remote

This page is for the Digital Lecturer.
You move the slides from a phone during Mass.
The Digital Minister gives you the link and the PIN.

## Before Mass

1. Open the link from the Digital Minister.
2. You see **Sacra Lux Remote**.
3. Tap the screen where it says **Tap anywhere to open the remote.**
4. You see the slide preview.
5. If you see **Enter the PIN to begin**, type the PIN.
6. The phone then shows the remote.

The PIN has 4 to 6 digits.
The Digital Minister sets the PIN.

If you see **Please enter the PIN.**, type the PIN.
If you see **Incorrect PIN.** or **Incorrect PIN. Alarm sounded.**, type the PIN again.
If you see **Unable to verify the PIN.**, ask the Digital Minister.

## During Mass

If the phone does not respond, read the message first.

1. If you see **Disconnected. Waiting for the server to reconnect.**, wait.
2. The preview updates when the phone connects again.
3. If the message stays, ask the Digital Minister.

Swipe left on the preview to show the next slide.
You see the next slide on the phone.
Swipe right on the preview to show the previous slide.

To show a chosen slide from the row under the preview:

1. Double-tap the slide.
2. The projector shows that slide.

Tap a row in the list to show that slide.
The list groups are **Pre-Mass Announcements**, **Gathering**, **Mass**, and **Post-Mass Announcements**.

## Lock the phone

1. Tap **Lock this remote**.
2. The phone shows **This remote is locked.**
3. Tap **Unlock the remote** when you need the remote again.
4. Type the PIN.

Ask the Digital Minister if you do not have the PIN.

The phone does not hide the projector.
Ask the Digital Minister to do that.
The steps are in the [user guide](user-guide.md#blackout-and-manual-navigation).
